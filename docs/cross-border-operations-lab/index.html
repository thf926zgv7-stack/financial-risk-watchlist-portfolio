Calculation boundary and formulas

Orders = baseline orders × (1 − price elasticity × price change). Revenue = orders × local unit price × FX. Contribution profit = revenue − product cost − logistics cost − platform fee. Fixed costs, tax, marketing spend, financing cost and contractual hedging are excluded.

Built for portfolio demonstration. Historical data: UCI Online Retail. Scenario values: illustrative and user-adjustable.
